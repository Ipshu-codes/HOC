Ward profile map of Ward No. 16, Kathmandu Metropolitan City, from the city's Urban Development Department: the ward boundary, roads, river, buildings and public services, with a legend Nani, the website's guide, greeting visitors with a namaste Emblem of Kathmandu Metropolitan City, to which Ward No. 16 belongs About Ward No. 16 Ward No. 16 is one of the 32 administrative wards of Kathmandu Metropolitan City, located in Kathmandu, Nepal. The ward encompasses the Balaju area and its surrounding localities, reflecting a rich blend of urban development, community life, and cultural heritage. It is home to historically and culturally significant sites such as Mhepi Ajima and Living Bhu-Samadhi Site of Mahayogi Shri Brahmaharidas Maharaj (Nanak Math), which preserve important religious traditions, local stories, and cultural practices. Beyond its historical significance, Ward No. 16 represents a living community where heritage, identity, and modern urban life coexist.
