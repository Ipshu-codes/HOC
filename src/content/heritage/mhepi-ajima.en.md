### What the inscriptions record

<!-- 20261104_084315.heic -->
- **Nepal Sambat 1120, Pohelathwa 12 (VS 2056, Magh 4)** — Shri Amrit Man Shrestha and Prithvi Kumari Shrestha, residents of Mhepi, offered a **two-tiered brass roof** to Shri Shri Shri Yogambar Jnaneshwari Mhepi Ajima, in memory of their ancestors, on the auspicious day of their Janku ceremony.

<!-- 20261104_083749.heic -->
- **Nepal Sambat 1120, Gunlathwa Navami, Tuesday (VS 2057, Shrawan)** — In memory of the late Mathuradas Shrestha and the late Vishnu Devi Shrestha, the family of Rajendra Das Shrestha laid white stone (marble) in the temple of Shri Yogambar Jnaneshwari. *(Inscription in Nepal Bhasa — translation to be verified.)*

<!-- 20261104_084318.heic -->
- **Nepal Sambat 1121, Thinla Dwadashi** — In memory of the late Gyanmaya Shakya, Buddharatna Shakya and Gyandevi Shakya, Shri Chaitya Ratna Shakya and Sanu Chhori Shakya offered metal-sheet work for the middle part of the main temple of Shri Yogambar Mhepi Ajima. *(Nepal Bhasa — translation to be verified.)*

<!-- 20261104_083919.heic -->
- **Nepal Sambat 1134, Thilla Dwadashi (VS 2070, Mangsir 29)** — Tirtharam Timila and Manik Laxmi Timila of Balaju offered metal-sheet work on both sides of Mhepi Ajima. *(Nepal Bhasa — translation to be verified.)*

<!-- 20261104_084559.heic (marble plaque, first panel) -->
- **The bhajan house** — A bhajan house was built in the Mhepi temple complex in **VS 2029**. When it fell into disrepair and rainwater began to disturb the daily bhajan-kirtan, the bhajan mandal decided on **VS 2068 Kartik 30** to rebuild it with devotees' support. The renovation was completed under the bhajan mandal's chair, Shri Ashtalal Maharjan; the commemorative plaque is dated **VS 2069 Jestha 6**.

## Architecture

<!-- Visual description from the field photographs, 4 Nov 2026 — no written source -->
*Described from the field photographs; [TODO: architectural history and dating].*

- The main temple has a **two-tiered roof** of dark carved wood over a lattice-screened sanctum, set in a **stone-paved courtyard**. A small gilded shrine flanked by stone lions stands in front of it.
- Inside, the deity's shrine is clad in **gilded and silver repoussé metalwork**, with a tiered metal roof and three **embossed cobra (naga) heads** beneath the inscription band.
- The grounds hold many smaller shrines: a red-brick **Ganesh shrine** with a gilded arch, a **tiered stone chaitya**, small red-painted shrines, carved stone reliefs set in niches, a stone pillar shrine with a metal parasol, and a small **water tank** with a reclining stone figure draped in yellow cloth.
- The complex is entered through an **ornate gate** under large old trees, its sign reading "म्हयपि अजिमा".

## Present Day

<!-- 20261104_090938.heic (notice board); 20261104_090028.heic; 20261104_084559.heic -->
The temple is looked after by the **Mhepi Conservation and Management Committee** (म्हेयपि संरक्षण तथा व्यवस्थापन समिति).

- **Sattals for ceremonies** — The committee rents out seven sattals (rest houses) in the temple grounds to devotees holding weddings (swayambar), bratabandha, puja, feasts and picnics. Fees on the notice board range from Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000, plus a Rs 300 cleaning fee; filming in the grounds is also charged. Advance booking is requested. [TODO: confirm current fees]
- **Bhajan mandal** — A bhajan mandal holds regular bhajan-kirtan in the bhajan house.
- **Meditation hall** — The grounds include the *Shantikar Dhyanagar Dhyanashram* (शान्तिकर ध्यानागार ध्यानाश्रम).
- The committee asks that **no stalls** be set up in front of the temple door.
